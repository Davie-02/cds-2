The process

Four steps from enquiry to licence.

  1. Book a slot

    Send a booking request online, WhatsApp us, or call to reserve your place on a course.

  2. Theory class

    Learn road signs, right-of-way rules and the highway code in small groups.

  3. Practical lessons

    One-on-one time behind the wheel with your dedicated instructor.

  4. Test day

    Sit a mock test with us first, then take your official test with confidence.
